ACCOUNT

Delete your account.

A Sinyal account is deleted inside the app. There is no separate web form. This page is the instruction the App Store asks for.

Steps

  • Open Sinyal and sign in.
  • Open Profile in the bottom bar, then Settings.
  • Tap Manage account → Delete account.
  • Confirm. This cannot be undone.

What is deleted

Your profile, photos, location pins, likes, comments, messages, and notifications. Copies on someone else’s device drop out of the chat history.

What is not deleted

Account content does not remain, except technical logs that must be kept briefly for a legal duty. Location pins already stop being used in Discover after 24 hours.
